Research

Computer architecture and machine learning systems

LLM serving simulation for hierarchical KV-cache systems

Feb 2026 – Present · Mentored by Shiyi Liu, a Ph.D. student advised by Prof. Yuan Xie

Extending vLLM-Sim, an LLM serving simulator built around vLLM, for LMCache-style hierarchical KV cache, so memory movement can be studied without running every operation on real devices.

Programming languages

LLVM SSA construction

Summer 2025 · Supervised by Prof. Jiasi Shen, with Ph.D. student Huiri Tan

An LLVM pass that rewrites IR into static single-assignment form: a dominance tree, phi functions on the dominance frontier, and a renaming walk.

Indirect-call program generation

Fall 2025 · With Ivan Lok and Puyang Zhang, supervised by Prof. Jiasi Shen, with Ph.D. student Huiri Tan

Two pipelines for compilable C++ programs with varied function-pointer and indirect-call patterns, for later WebAssembly reverse engineering. Dataset transformation often inserted pointers incidental to the source. Skeleton generation and reconstruction compiled all 300 pilot programs.

Human–computer interaction

CoNarrate

Jan 2025 – Sep 2025 · With Hei Ching Iu and Ph.D. student Dingdong Liu · Prof. Xiaojuan Ma

An intake assistant for asynchronous telehealth that elicits missing clinical detail before the clinician sees the note. In a 46-participant study the median consultation fell from 22 rounds to 14. I assisted in building the front end, took part in setting the study method, and helped run the user studies. The paper is under Publications.

Automated cognitive tests

Jan 2025 – Sep 2025 · With Hei Ching Iu · Prof. Xiaojuan Ma

An ablation of prompt structure for a spoken grocery-shopping cognitive screening task, on GPT-4o and Qwen2.5, including transcripts that contain speech-recognition errors.

Newcomers on RedNote

Jan 2025 – Sep 2025 · With Kangyu Yuan, Hanfang Lyu, Ziqi Pan, Yuanhao Zhang, Junze Li, Bingcan Guo, Jiaxiong Hu, and Qingyu Guo · Prof. Xiaojuan Ma

An analysis of 3,510 RedNote posts from the January 2025 arrival of TikTok users, identifying twelve strategies in the initial cross-cultural exchange. The paper is under Publications.

Coursework

Hardware–software co-optimization on Gemmini

Spring 2026 · ELEC 5140 Advanced Computer Architecture · with Puyang Zhang

Hardware parameter sweeps and manual loop tiling for MLP and CNN kernels on Gemmini. A 32×32 array together with a tile that fits the accumulator gave a 4× speedup on the large convolution. A larger TLB slowed the automatic schedule.

Amy compiler

Spring 2026 · COMP 4121 Modern Compiler Construction

An interpreter and a WebAssembly compiler for Amy, sharing a front end of longest-match lexing, LL(1) parsing, name analysis, and constraint-based type checking.

User-Space TCP Implementation

Fall 2025 · ELEC 3120 Computer Networks

A user-space TCP over UDP. I measured delivery from the first write until the server had the file, replaced stop-and-wait with a sliding window, recovered loss with fast retransmit under Reno, and used TCP CUBIC for the congestion window.
