CoNarrate

Jan 2025 – Sep 2025 · With Hei Ching Iu and Ph.D. student Dingdong Liu · Prof. Xiaojuan Ma

A lot of remote care happens by message. The patient writes down what is wrong, and a doctor reads it later. That first message is often too thin to act on, and the doctor spends the next few messages pulling the rest out. CoNarrate is an intake assistant that asks follow-up questions before the doctor sees the note.

On this paper I built the front end of that conversational agent, took part in the discussions that set the study method, and helped run the user studies.

The paper will appear at UIST 2026. The author list is on Publications.
