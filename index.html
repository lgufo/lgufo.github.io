Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

I am Li Zhang (张力), an undergraduate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), and this term an exchange student in the Department of Computer Science at ETH Zurich. I have broad research interests in computer systems, spanning computer architecture, distributed systems, efficient machine learning systems, high-performance computing, computer networking, cloud and edge computing, and programming languages. I am always open to exploring new research directions and problems in computer systems.

Most recently, I have been working with Shiyi Liu, a Ph.D. student advised by Prof. Yuan Xie, on vLLM-Sim and LMCache. Before that, I worked with Prof. Jiasi Shen and Ph.D. student Huiri Tan on an LLVM SSA pass and indirect-call program generation. Earlier, I conducted research in the HKUST HCI Initiative under Prof. Xiaojuan Ma, contributing to projects on asynchronous telehealth, automated cognitive tests, and online community interactions. My first research experience was in medical imaging with Prof. Xiaomeng Li.
