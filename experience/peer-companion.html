Peer Companion

HKUST Counseling and Wellness Center · Dec 2024 – Present

Peer Companions are trained students in the Counseling and Wellness Center’s Peer Support Community. They keep other HKUST students company, in person and online, and take part in outreach that makes mental wellness easier to talk about: peer chats, campus-wide events, and support during exam weeks. The Center trains them in those support skills before they start.

I joined in December 2024. My part has been the everyday side of that work. Peer coffee chats are short conversations with whoever sits down. Caring Day is a big event in the main building atrium: small games for letting off stress or getting closer to how you feel, with gifts and snacks, so students can enjoy themselves and open up a little further. During exam week we mainly set out snacks and desserts, so people passing by can recharge.
